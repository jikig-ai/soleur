Running a company alone means making hundreds of decisions every week. As AI agentic coding tools evolve, solo founders often find themselves facing a frustrating dilemma: every time a faster model or a more powerful harness launches, switching means leaving your company's context and workflow behind.

Your marketing guidelines, your legal guardrails, your financial models, and your product roadmap are scattered across chat tabs. You start from scratch on every new tool.

Soleur was built to solve this problem. Today, we are bringing Soleur natively to [Google Antigravity CLI](https://github.com/google/antigravity).

## Your Company's Memory Stays Where It Belongs: With You

Soleur turns a solo founder into a full organization — AI specialists across all eight departments: engineering, marketing, legal, finance, operations, product, sales, and support, all sharing one persistent memory.

Instead of locking your business context inside one vendor's proprietary system, Soleur stores your company's institutional memory as plain files in your own repository. Every decision, brand choice, and engineering standard is remembered and shared across all departments.

With native Google Antigravity CLI support, you can now run that entire organization on Google's agentic harness:

- **No context loss:** Your marketing agent knows your brand guide. Your legal agent references your compliance standards. Your engineering team follows your architectural rules.
- **Asynchronous delegation:** Complex tasks run in the background with native reactive wakeups, alerting you only when input is needed or work is ready.
- **Freedom to choose:** Run on Claude Code, Grok Build, Codex, Devin CLI, OpenCode, or Google Antigravity CLI — and switch between them without losing a single past decision.

Running a company alone shouldn't mean doing everything alone. And it shouldn't mean being locked into a single AI vendor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Soleur run on Google Antigravity CLI?

Yes. Soleur installs natively on Google Antigravity CLI (`agy`), allowing you to run your full AI team across engineering, marketing, legal, finance, operations, product, sales, and support directly within Google's agentic harness.

### Which models can I use with Google Antigravity?

Google Antigravity CLI connects natively to Google's Gemini models, including Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini Flash, giving your AI specialists fast inference and large context windows while keeping your business memory stored locally.

### Do I lose my company's memory if I switch tools?

No. Your company's memory lives in plain markdown files inside your own Git repository. You can run Soleur on Google Antigravity CLI today and switch between any supported coding harness without losing your business context, brand guidelines, or operational history.
