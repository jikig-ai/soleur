You decided to run your company with AI agents. Then you opened a search tab and found two very different answers: platforms that hand you the parts to build agents, and products that hand you a team that already works. Both are called "AI agents." Only one of them gets your contracts reviewed, your pricing researched and your launch planned this week.

This post helps you pick the one that fits the way you work.

## The Two Things People Call "AI Agents"

An **AI agent platform** (often called an agent framework) is a toolkit. You choose what each agent does, connect it to your tools, test how the agents hand work to each other, and fix them when they break. The toolkit is flexible, and the assembly is your job.

An **AI organization** is the finished team. The agents for legal, marketing, finance, product and operations already exist, already share what they learn, and are ready on day one. Your job is to decide, not to build.

A useful comparison: a platform is a box of kitchen equipment, and an organization is a restaurant that already has a cook. If your goal is to invent a new kind of kitchen, buy the equipment. If your goal is to serve dinner tonight, you want the cook.

## The Question That Decides It

Ask yourself: **"Is my bottleneck the tooling, or the amount of work?"**

Most solo founders are not short of tooling. They are short of hours. The contract still needs a read, the competitor page needs updating, the invoice reminder needs sending, and the founder is the only person in the company. A toolkit adds one more project to that list: building the thing that does the work.

## Where an Agent Platform Is the Right Pick

Choose a platform when:

- You are a developer, and building the system is the point or the product.
- You need behavior no ready-made product offers, such as a very specific data pipeline.
- You want full control over every step and are happy to maintain it.

If that describes you, a platform is a good investment, and we say so plainly.

## Where an AI Organization Is the Right Pick

Choose an organization when:

- You run a company and need work done across several departments, not one clever workflow.
- You do not want to spend your first month building before you get your first result.
- You want what the team learns on Monday, such as a legal constraint or a brand decision, to inform what it does on Friday.

That last point matters most. A team that forgets everything between tasks makes you repeat yourself forever. A team that keeps a shared, readable record of your decisions gets more useful the longer you work together.

## What You Still Decide

Neither option removes you from the company. With Soleur, an AI organization for solo founders, the agents prepare, draft, research and check. You approve the things that carry your name: what you sign, what you publish, what you spend. That is the line between a team that works for you and a tool that acts without you.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between an AI agent platform and an AI organization?**

An AI agent platform is a toolkit for building your own agents, so you design, connect and maintain them. An AI organization is a ready-made team of agents covering the departments of a company, so you start giving it work immediately.

**Q: Can I build a company with AI agents if I cannot code?**

Yes, if you choose a ready-made AI organization. Agent platforms generally assume you can build and debug software. An organization is meant to be directed in plain language, with you making the final calls.

**Q: Do I need an agent platform and an AI organization together?**

Usually not. Most solo founders need work done across departments, which an AI organization covers. A developer with an unusual workflow might add a custom-built agent later.

**Q: What is Company-as-a-Service?**

[Company-as-a-Service]({{ site.url }}/company-as-a-service/) is the idea that a founder gets the capabilities of a whole company, such as legal, marketing and finance work, as one platform they direct, instead of hiring or assembling each function.
