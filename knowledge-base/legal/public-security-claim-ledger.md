# Public security claim ledger

> Internal working ledger. Not a statement by Jikigai about product security, not a warranty, not a published security claim. A status here gates what may be said elsewhere; it is not a representation to anyone. Validity: only as of origin/main commit f05eaab78faec600bb8a2c763891bf79a34d748b (pinned 2026-11-03); every evidence token must be re-resolved before any reliance. The repository is public, so this file is readable by anyone; it is a claimability register, not a publication.

Scope: three claim topics, namely isolation (prefix I), egress (prefix E) and human approval (prefix A). A row is a topic descriptor and the control that would measure it, never a sentence meant for any public surface.

## Legend

**Statuses.** Part 1 rows take exactly one of `measured`, `dark`, `not-shipped`, `advisory`. Part 2 rows take exactly one of `published-measured`, `published-unverified`.

- `measured`: measured within the descriptor's scope by a named instrument, on a stated host class, at a stated commit, with the result recorded in the evidence cell. A test arm that was skipped is `skipped`, not `measured`.
- `dark`: the control is flag-off or not enabled for anyone. On for internal or test use only is `dark` with the segment named. On for any external organisation is not `dark`. An upward flip needs a cited artifact, never a flag read alone.
- `not-shipped`: no code exists, or only a recorded decision, or an entry not yet written.
- `advisory`: the control exists or runs, but no measurement of it as a control is cited. The sub-case (`non-gating` or `unmeasured`) is named in the evidence cell. An `advisory` row is never claimable and leaves `advisory` only through a cited measurement at a path that resolves.
- `published-measured` (Part 2): every mapped Part 1 row is `measured`, the statement's scope is no wider than theirs, and it states every residual tag. Otherwise `published-unverified`. For a `disclosure`, `published-measured` means only that the evidence supports the disclosed limit; it is not a security claim.

**Measurement states.** `not-run`, `skipped` and `blind` are three different states and none of them is `measured`. A platform step conclusion is not a measurement: a `measured` cell cites the measurement artifact (run identity and the required result fields), never the step verdict.

**Flag column.** The last column of a Part 1 row is exactly `yes` or `no`. `yes` means do not claim. An empty or unparseable cell means `yes`. `no` is reserved for a `measured` row with no tags and host class `production-image`; none exists today. Every `yes` row carries `[prod-unmeasured: <the dimension nobody measured>]` and/or `[residual: <pointer>]` in its evidence cell. A `no` row carrying either tag counts as `yes`.

**Measured rows carry** the owning decision record's status word quoted verbatim, `host class: CI-runner`, `dev-host` or `production-image` (missing or unrecognised means `CI-runner`), the measured-commit SHA, and a non-empty list of instrumented paths.

**Evidence tokens.** Backticked repository paths (resolved with `git cat-file -e origin/main:<path>`), `gh:pr/<n>`, `gh:issue/<n>`, `gh:run/<id>` (resolved with `gh run view <id> --json event,headSha,conclusion,workflowName`; a run that no longer resolves counts as no measurement cited), and the plain-text marker `unverified: <what>`. Decision records are cited as full paths under `knowledge-base/engineering/architecture/decisions/`. Residuals are cited by pointer only.

## Publication Criterion

A sentence about isolation, egress or human approval may enter any public surface only if every condition below holds. Part 1 statuses apply to Part 1 rows and Part 2 statuses to Part 2 rows.

- C1. Every mapped Part 1 row is `measured`; the owning decision record status is exactly `accepted`; no mapped row carries a `[prod-unmeasured]` tag; the host class is no narrower than the sentence's scope (a statement about hosted production behaviour needs `production-image`); the sentence states every `[residual]` tag of its rows; its scope is no wider than the rows' scope. When a sentence maps to several rows, the weakest row decides.
- C2. Evidence resolves and has not decayed: `git diff <measured-commit>..origin/main --stat -- <instrumented paths>` prints nothing at the publishing change's base, and every `gh:run` token still resolves.
- C3. The CLO has signed the wording; sign-off at flag-on stays with the human principal.
- C4. The Article 30 technical-measure entry, the data protection disclosure and the Privacy Policy edits, with their mirrors and the legal-document sha file, ship in the same change.
- C5. The publishing change adds its own claim guard. The precedent is `scripts/image-cve-public-claim-guard.test.sh` and the claim-guard decision in `knowledge-base/engineering/architecture/decisions/ADR-281-image-cve-scan-advisory-first-promoted-on-measured-fp-criterion.md`; a passing exit of that script shows nothing about any non-image claim.
- C6. The statuses `advisory`, `dark`, `not-shipped` and `published-unverified` never pass. `not-run`, `skipped` and `blind` never count as `measured`. A `published-measured` disclosure never clears an assurance.
