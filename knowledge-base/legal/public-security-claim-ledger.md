## Legend

**Statuses.** Part 1 rows take exactly one of `measured`, `dark`, `not-shipped`, `advisory`. Part 2 rows take exactly one of `published-measured`, `published-unverified`.

- `measured`: measured within the descriptor's scope by a named instrument, on a stated host class, at a stated commit, with the result recorded in the evidence cell. A test arm that was skipped is `skipped`, not `measured`. A preparer's reproduction run (commands in Method) measures the instrument at the pinned commit but is not independently resolvable: C1 and C2 require a resolvable `gh:run` or a re-run at the publishing change's base before any sentence relies on it.
- `dark`: the control is flag-off or not enabled for anyone. On for internal or test use only is `dark` with the segment named. On for any external organisation is not `dark`. An upward flip needs a cited artifact, never a flag read alone. A `dark` row cites a dated flag read in its evidence cell; without one the row is `advisory`.
- `not-shipped`: no code exists, or only a recorded decision, or an entry not yet written.
- `advisory`: a control or document is cited in the repository; whether it operates, or is enforced rather than instructed, is not established. The sub-case (`non-gating` or `unmeasured`) is named in the evidence cell. `advisory` does not mean recommended and does not mean live. An `advisory` row is never claimable and leaves `advisory` only through a cited measurement at a path that resolves.
- `published-measured` (Part 2): the statement meets every condition of C1 at the pinned commit; otherwise `published-unverified`. `published-unverified` records that no mapped row meets C1 at the pinned commit; it describes the ledger's evidence and is not a finding about the statement. A `disclosure` is a statement of a limit or risk. The branch under which a disclosure is `published-measured` when the evidence supports the disclosed limit has not been applied in this pass: every statement, disclosures included, is recorded `published-unverified`.

**Measurement states.** `not-run`, `skipped` and `blind` are three different states and none of them is `measured`. A platform step conclusion is not a measurement: a `measured` cell cites the measurement artifact (run identity and the required result fields, or for a `dev-host` row the preparer's reproduction record in the evidence cell), never the step verdict.

**Flag column.** The last column of a Part 1 row is exactly `yes` or `no`. `yes` means do not claim. An empty or unparseable cell means `yes`. `no` is reserved for a `measured` row with no tags, owning decision record status `accepted` and host class `production-image`; none exists today. Every `yes` row carries `[prod-unmeasured: <the dimension nobody measured>]` and/or `[residual: <pointer>]` in its evidence cell. A `no` row carrying either tag counts as `yes`.

**Measured rows carry** the owning decision record's status word quoted verbatim (the front-matter `status:` key, case-normalised; a later section or addendum of the same record at a lower status is recorded as a residual), `host class: CI-runner`, `dev-host` or `production-image` (missing or unrecognised means `CI-runner`), the measured-commit SHA, and a non-empty list of instrumented paths and instruments. `production-image` means the instrument was executed inside the deployed image under the production run tuple, with the artifact cited. `CI-runner` and `dev-host` are narrower than `production-image` and not comparable to each other. A customer-machine scope has no host class that can meet C1 until one is defined.

**Evidence tokens.** Backticked repository paths (resolved with `git cat-file -e origin/main:<path>`), `gh:pr/<n>`, `gh:issue/<n>`, `gh:run/<id>` (resolved with `gh run view <id> --json event,headSha,conclusion,workflowName`; a run that no longer resolves counts as no measurement cited), and the plain-text marker `unverified: <what>`. Decision records are cited as full paths under `knowledge-base/engineering/architecture/decisions/`. Residuals are cited by pointer only.

## Publication Criterion

A sentence asserting or implying a security property of the product, including on the isolation, egress and human-approval topics, may enter any public surface only if every condition below holds. Part 1 statuses apply to Part 1 rows and Part 2 statuses to Part 2 rows.

- C1. Every mapped Part 1 row is `measured`; the owning decision record status is exactly `accepted`; no mapped row carries a `[prod-unmeasured]` tag; the host class is no narrower than the sentence's scope (a statement about hosted production behaviour needs `production-image`); the sentence states every `[residual]` tag of its rows; its scope is no wider than the rows' scope. When a sentence maps to several rows, the weakest row decides. The owning record's status is the status of the section or addendum that owns the control, with no later section of the same record at a lower status. A nondeterministic probe needs more than one recorded run or a recorded pass rate. Rows are read at the publishing change's merge base: a change that edits a row's status, tag or evidence cannot also publish a sentence that relies on that row.
- C2. Evidence resolves and has not decayed: `git diff <measured-commit>..origin/main --stat -- <instrumented paths> <instruments> <decision record path>` and the dependency manifest and lockfile of the instrumented package print nothing at the publishing change's base, every `gh:run` token still resolves, and the instruments are re-run at that base with 0 skipped and cited by `gh:run`.
- C3. The human principal has signed the wording after a CLO review recorded in a committed file. An agent-authored CLO record alone does not satisfy C3, and a committed CLO counsel-review attestation (the ship-gate review) is required for any sentence about credential or tenant isolation; the human principal decides whether outside counsel is also engaged. Compliance-posture rows that gate the same wording are additional required gates.
- C4. The Article 30 technical-measure entry, the data protection disclosure and the Privacy Policy edits, with their mirrors and the legal-document sha file, ship in the same change.
- C5. The publishing change adds its own claim guard. The precedent is `scripts/image-cve-public-claim-guard.test.sh` and the claim-guard decision in `knowledge-base/engineering/architecture/decisions/ADR-281-image-cve-scan-advisory-first-promoted-on-measured-fp-criterion.md`; a passing exit of that script shows nothing about any non-image claim. The guard parses this ledger, fails on any `yes` row the sentence relies on, and is registered as a required check.
- C6. The statuses `advisory`, `dark`, `not-shipped` and `published-unverified` never pass. `not-run`, `skipped` and `blind` never count as `measured`. A `published-measured` disclosure never clears an assurance.

Before any credential-isolation claim, condition C3 of `knowledge-base/legal/audits/2026-10-counsel-review-9601.md` also applies: the claim is limited to the entry's text, and the canonical document, its mirror and the sha file move together. A change that publishes such a sentence would need CPO sign-off and CMO consultation.
