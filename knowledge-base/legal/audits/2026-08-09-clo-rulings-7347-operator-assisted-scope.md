## RULING

**Yes — a `gdpr-policy.md` §3.14 entry is required, and yes — the Art. 21(4) right to object must be
brought explicitly.** Neither is optional and neither is a counsel question.

**Rationale.** The review under-states the defect. `gdpr-policy.md` §2.2's operator-assisted bullet as

# D4 — The retention sections were not swept

## RULING

**Yes — both must be swept in this PR: `privacy-policy.md` §7 bullet 1 and `gdpr-policy.md` §8.1.** No
retention figure is published; the qualitative fact is.

**Rationale.** These two sentences are the pair that most directly misleads on the one point a data
subject would act on. "You control its retention and deletion entirely" and "Soleur does not impose
any retention period on locally stored data" are true of the plugin-local configuration and

controller's own records say otherwise. Annex §6.3 records that Jikigai "**cannot** accelerate

or not**." The qualitative fact is not C6-contingent — on Commercial or Consumer Terms alike, a
Jikigai-keyed egress leaves content with a third party for a period the User does not set. Only the
number is contingent, and A2 bars the number regardless. Publishing the fact without the figure is
exactly what A2 permits and what honesty requires.

## Adjudication A — the two indented Scope blocks MOVE. My row 21 was wrong.

**RULING: move both flush-left. Row 21 is superseded by rows 21a–21c. The implementer was right to

**The moves are required on legal-effect grounds, not typographic ones.** Under CommonMark a

renders as a rider on bullet (a) in §3.1 and on bullet (b) in §10.1, and the qualification attaches to
