# Competitive Intelligence Report

## Executive Summary

This is the first full-landscape scan (all six tiers) since the report's inception; prior scans covered Tier 0 and Tier 3 only. **Three material shifts dominate.** First, **Polsia's trajectory reversed hard**: against the prior scan's "ARR fell to $450K / possible churn event" read, Polsia raised **$30M at a $250M valuation (Sound Ventures lead, True Ventures participating, May 2026)** and newer third-party reports cite ~$10M ARR / 7,600 customers / 85% month-two retention — though figures across sources remain contradictory (a Feb 2026 Mixergy interview implied ~$689K run-rate). The autonomous-CaaS thesis Soleur competes against is now venture-validated at scale, not fading. Second, **the plugin-layer competitor `alirezarezvani/claude-skills` grew from 235 to 337 skills (5,200+ stars, 12+ AI tools, 30+ agents, 70+ commands)** — it is iterating faster than Soleur's headline counts and remains the closest structural match in the landscape. Third, **platform billing across Tier 0 shifted to metered/credit models simultaneously** (GitHub Copilot AI Credits June 1 with 10–50x agentic cost spikes and backlash; OpenAI Codex Pro promo expiry June 1; Notion Custom Agents to $10/1,000 credits, Business/Enterprise only) — a pricing-environment signal Soleur's monetization plan must read against.

A NanoCorp data-reconciliation flag emerged: the marketing site (now confirming parent **Phospho Inc.**, Wilmington DE) lists Google Search Ads as "**Coming Soon**," contradicting the 2026-06-08 business-validation.md / prior CI intake that described ads + Vercel + Stripe as already shipping. The self-reported "$740k ARR in 33 days" also conflicts with a newer "$193k ARR in 3 days" founder claim and a ~$264 user-revenue leaderboard. All NanoCorp metrics remain marketing/unverified and now mutually inconsistent — see Cascade Results for the operator reconciliation note. Soleur's durable differentiation across all tiers is unchanged: the **workflow lifecycle (brainstorm → plan → implement → review → compound) with named per-stage artifacts**, the **git-tracked knowledge base as canonical memory**, and **founder-in-the-loop** vs. the fully-autonomous CaaS pack.

### Tier 0 Key Takeaways

##### Recommendations (ICP-expansion framing per CMO)

Grouped by narrative, not announced as individual skill-adds. **All advisory — nothing has been filed; the operator decides what gets issued.**

- **"The founder can actually do the thing"** — bundles G1 + B9 + B12, with G5 as a cheap rider. Soleur's operator-step machinery is an *accounting* system: `wg-block-pr-ready-on-undeferred-operator-steps` blocks a PR whose body declares an operator step without a tracked automation-gap issue, and `operator-digest` harvests `action-required` issues weekly. Every part of that answers *"has this step been deferred correctly?"* — none answers *"can the founder perform it?"* The deliverable is still an `AC-PM3` line in a PR body, and `ship` itself already concedes *"a PR body is not an operator-visible surface."* `wizard` supplies the missing delivery half, and Soleur has **already written the rules that demand it** and never built the template. Founder outcome: manual steps become a script the founder runs, a reversibility verdict they can read, and a map that tells them which of 99 skills to reach for. Suggested landing PR: **"feat(ship): wizard-generated operator bootstrap scripts + Merge Danger block"**.
- **"Prove it, don't assert it"** — bundles B1 + B2 + B3 + B7. Soleur's deepest cultural strength is refusing unmeasured claims (`hr-no-dashboard-eyeball-pull-data-yourself`, `cq-assert-anchor-not-bare-token`'s mutation clause, ADR-151's *"a green gate that cannot fail is worse than no gate"*). That discipline is fully present in Soleur's **test and rule** surfaces and almost entirely **absent from its debugging and scaffolding** surfaces — 0 of 6 diagnosing-bugs mechanics, and a constraint gate proved to bite only in its own hermetic suite, never in the repo it is installed into. This is the bundle with the best fit-to-existing-values ratio in the audit. Founder outcome: "we fixed it" becomes a red-then-green command the founder can re-run themselves. Suggested landing PR: **"feat(reproduce-bug): red-capable loop gate, tagged instrumentation, seam-absence finding"**.
- **"The founder's own words, and their durable no"** — bundles G2 + G3 + B11, with G4 adjacent. A solo non-technical operator has no institutional memory except what the system keeps. Today Soleur keeps *decisions* (151+ ADRs), *learnings* (6,610 KB files) and *deferrals* (`deferred-scope-out`) — but keeps **no glossary** and **no record of what was decided against**, so 68 agents each invent their own vocabulary and every re-arriving feature request is re-litigated from scratch. Founder outcome: the business speaks one language, and "no" only has to be said once. Suggested landing PR: **"feat(kb): domain glossary + rejected-request register wired into triage"**.
- **"Write once, load less"** — bundles B5 + B6 + the user-invoked/model-invoked pattern. Soleur measures its always-loaded budget better than this peer does (`B_ALWAYS ≤ 46000` pre-commit, a 600-byte per-rule cap, `rules_unused_over_8w`), yet ADR-151 records only ~1,453 bytes of headroom to warn — "roughly four average-sized rules to the warn tier and nine to hard reject" — and its own addendum concedes that telemetry-driven retirement is structurally unavailable, leaving *migration* as the only live shrink lever. The peer supplies a **second** lever Soleur has not considered: the invocation axis. Marking genuinely human-only skills user-invoked (candidates: `flag-*`, `cron-*`, `provision-*`, `invoice`, `admin-ip-refresh`) returns their descriptions to the budget **entirely** rather than trimming them word by word. Pair with the negation rewrite, A/B'd through `eval-harness`. Founder outcome: the assistant stays sharp as the product grows, instead of degrading under its own instructions. Suggested landing PR: **"refactor(skills): invocation-axis budget relief + positive-phrasing rule rewrite (eval-gated)"**.
- **"Work too big to hold in one head"** — B8 alone, lowest priority and smallest scope. Deliberately narrow: import fog-of-war into `product-roadmap` and native blocking edges into the `plan`/`roadmap`/`drain` triangle. Do **not** import `wayfinder` wholesale — it is the peer's own most cognitively demanding flow, aimed at a senior engineer charting a greenfield build, and Soleur's `plan`→`work`→`ship` pipeline with its anti-bypass rule is the better default for this ICP. Founder outcome: the founder sees the known-unknowns in their own roadmap. Suggested landing PR: **"feat(product-roadmap): Not-Yet-Specified section + native issue blocking"**.
