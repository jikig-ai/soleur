## Discord

Two things get called "AI agents," and they are not the same purchase.

An agent platform is a toolkit. You design the agents, connect them, test them and fix them when they break.

An AI organization is a finished team. Legal, marketing, finance, product and operations already exist, share what they learn, and are ready on day one.

The deciding question for a solo founder: is your bottleneck the tooling, or the amount of work? Most of us are short of hours, not tools. A toolkit adds one more project to the pile.

You still decide what you sign, publish and spend. The team prepares, drafts and checks.

Full breakdown, with a side-by-side table: https://soleur.ai/blog/agent-platform-vs-ai-organization/?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=agent-platform-vs-ai-organization
