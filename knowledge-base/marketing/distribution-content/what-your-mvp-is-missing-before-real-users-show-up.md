## LinkedIn Personal

Your product works. You have clicked through it a hundred times, and it does what you built it to do.

Then someone who is not you is about to sign up, with their email address and maybe their card, and a quiet question shows up: what haven't I checked?

Clicking around more won't answer it. The things that hurt a young product are rarely on the screen. A password saved in your code. No test that tells you a change broke sign-up. No alert when the site goes down at 3 a.m.

What a founder needs here is a list, not a grade. A score invites the wrong question ("am I a 72?"), and no automated check can promise what a certificate promises. A plain list of gaps, in the order they matter, lets you pick the next three things to fix and ignore the rest for now.

That is what Soleur's launch readiness check produces. It runs in your own session, writes one report, and says up front that it is a findings list rather than a security review. Secret findings are counts only. Turning the gaps into tasks in your GitHub project is opt-in, and it is built to ask first, before it files anything.

I wrote up what it covers, what a clean report does not tell you, and how the filing step is built to ask first: https://soleur.ai/blog/what-your-mvp-is-missing-before-real-users-show-up/?utm_source=linkedin-personal&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=what-your-mvp-is-missing-before-real-users-show-up
