**Title:** Agent kit or ready-made AI team? How I think about it as a solo founder

**Body:**

Building your own AI agents sounds like freedom. It also adds jobs: designing each specialist, connecting them, keeping them current, and reviewing what they produce. If you already do eight jobs, that can quietly become the ninth.

The alternative is a ready-made team that shares one memory of your company, where you stay the decision-maker. I wrote up four questions that help decide between the two, and a hybrid rule: custom agents only for what is unique to your business.

## LinkedIn Personal

Most solo founders I talk to don't have an AI problem. They have a coordination problem.

One AI chat for copy, another for contracts, a third for the spreadsheet. Each is smart. Each starts every conversation knowing nothing about the business. So the founder becomes the relay, pasting context from one tool to the next.

That is the real difference between an AI agent platform and an AI organization. A platform is a kit: you design the specialists and the shared memory. An organization is the finished team, with one memory of your company, while you stay the decision-maker.

I wrote up four questions to help choose, plus a simple rule: build custom agents only for what is unique to your business.
