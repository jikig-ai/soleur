## LinkedIn Company Page

Soleur now runs natively on Google Antigravity CLI — our sixth supported agentic harness alongside Claude Code, Grok Build, Codex, Devin CLI, and OpenCode.

For solo founders, adopting a new AI model or development tool usually comes at a steep price: losing the context and operational memory built up over months of work. Brand guidelines, compliance rules, financial projections, and product specs remain stranded in disconnected chat windows.

Soleur eliminates this tradeoff by decoupling your company's institutional memory from any single AI harness or model provider. Every specialist agent works from plain-text documents versioned in your Git repository.

With native Antigravity support, founders can now orchestrate an entire 8-department organization — engineering, marketing, legal, finance, operations, product, sales, and support — directly on Google's agentic harness. You gain the speed and long context windows of Gemini models, combined with native asynchronous delegation and persistent company memory.

Your company's memory stays where it belongs: with you.
