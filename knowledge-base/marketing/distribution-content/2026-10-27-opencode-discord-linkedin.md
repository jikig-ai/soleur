## Discord

Soleur now runs on OpenCode (@opencode) — the fifth supported harness alongside Claude Code, Grok Build, Codex, and Devin CLI.

Run the full 67-agent, 103-skill AI organization on open-weights models or any BYOK provider while keeping your company memory in plain files you own.

One command to install: npx @soleur/opencode install (or bunx @soleur/opencode install). It vendors skills, agent stubs, /go routing, and shell guards into ~/.config/opencode/soleur/ so they survive npx cache pruning.

OpenCode supports local models through Ollama, llama.cpp, and LM Studio. Your plain-file knowledge base stays intact when you switch supported providers.

## LinkedIn Company Page

Soleur now runs on OpenCode (@opencode) — the fifth supported harness alongside Claude Code, Grok Build, Codex, and Devin CLI.

Soleur gives a single founder the leverage of a full organization — eight departments covering engineering, marketing, legal, finance, operations, product, sales, and support. Each department has specialist AI agents that share one knowledge base. Every decision, brand guide, legal audit, financial model, and engineering plan is stored as a plain document in your own Git repository.

The key architectural decision is separation. Your company's knowledge and your AI team's workflows are not tied to any single model or harness. They live in your repository as plain files. You can run Soleur on Claude Code, Grok Build, Codex, Devin CLI, or OpenCode — and switch between them without losing a single past decision.

For founders who want maximum control, the arrival of capable open-weights models changes the equation entirely. You can now run Soleur's full AI organization on models you host yourself or access through any provider you choose.
