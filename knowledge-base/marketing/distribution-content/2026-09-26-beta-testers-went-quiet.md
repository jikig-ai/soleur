## IndieHackers

**Title:** My first beta testers went quiet — so I rebuilt the program to answer back

**Body:**

Four people agreed to try my product. Three weeks later: silence. I couldn't tell who actually used it, and asking felt desperate.

The problem: getting testers to say yes is easy. Knowing what they did is hard, and most of us solve it with hope. So I rebuilt the program around three pieces — testers tagged inside the product, check-ins scheduled before they're needed, and a private metadata-only activity log that lives on the tester's own machine (they read it, they share it if they want, one switch turns it off).

The last part is the one people don't expect: measuring testers without collecting their data. The log never contains what they typed — only what the product did.
