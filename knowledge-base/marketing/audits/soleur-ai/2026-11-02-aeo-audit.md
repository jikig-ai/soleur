**Overall AEO score: 74/100 (SAP headline) -- Grade C.** Prior (2026-10-26): 76 B+. Delta: **-2** (Structure -1, Authority -1, Presence 0).

What moved this week:

- **No prior finding was fixed.** All 19 tracked items from 2026-10-26 are unchanged in source. The core pages (`/`, `/pricing/`, `/vision/`, `/getting-started/`, both compare pages) still carry `last_updated: 2026-10-22` and the same text.

That post will show a 17-day-ahead date over a claim that may already be stale when it appears.
- **The citation gap spread (N6, #11135).** 2 more arc posts shipped (`2026-11-19-first-we-made-green-mean-something.md`, `2026-12-03-alarm-passed-every-rehearsal-never-rings.md`), and neither links an outside source. 11 of the 13 posts dated 2026-10-23 or later cite no outside source. The 2 exceptions are the hub (Osmani) and the vendor lock-in post (OpenCode provider docs).
- **New entity drift: the harness list now has two versions (N10, NEW).** `/getting-started/` adds "Google Antigravity CLI" as a 6th harness. The homepage, `/pricing/`, `/vision/`, `/community/`, and both compare pages still list 5. `/getting-started/` contradicts itself: the top of the page lists 6 harnesses and its FAQ lists 5. This reverses part of last cycle's main entity-clarity gain (N4).
- **A thin announcement post shipped (N11, NEW).** `2026-10-26-run-your-ai-company-on-google-antigravity.md` has 35 lines, no FAQ (visible or schema), and no outside link (not even Google's own Antigravity docs). Its department list names 6 departments while its meta description says "8-department".
- **What the new posts do well.** The 11-19 and 12-03 posts have visible FAQs (4 and 3 questions), measured and qualified numbers ("1.88 times faster", "on a small, controlled set of checks, on the opt-in path"; "twelve merges, zero broken mains"), and explicit "What this does not prove" / "The honest limits" sections. That is the strongest first-hand Experience signal on the site. The vendor lock-in post cites a primary source with a specific number ("more than 75 providers").
- **Presence is flat for a sixth cycle (14/25).** The citation tracker still has `last_updated: 2026-06-15` and a single baseline run block. Today is the **20th** Monday missed.

Net: shipping is fast and the new posts are well built, but nothing was repaired, two regressions widened (future dates, citation-free arc), and one new contradiction appeared (harness list). The SAP total drops 2 points into C.
