## Brand-guide note

The Blog channel note (`brand-guide.md` `### Blog`) bars engineering metrics and terminal commands in founder posts. It also says a refresh of an existing post updates facts only. All recommendations above fit that rule. Specificity for blog posts should come from founder-facing figures (departments, hours, decisions) and named outside sources, not test or PR counts. N12's fix favors removal over adding a more exact count.
