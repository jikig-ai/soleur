## Critical Issues

### P0: `future-dated-posts-published-early` (#11132, carried, extended)

- **Where:**
  - `eleventy.config.js`: no publish filter.
  - `plugins/soleur/docs/blog/blog.json`: no `eleventyComputed`.
  - `.github/workflows/deploy-docs.yml`: no `schedule:` trigger.
  - The 8 posts in the table above.
- **Evidence:** see the tracked-issues table. Eleventy builds every template whatever its date, so every post is in `collections.blog` and `collections.all`, in the Atom feed (`limit: 20`), on `/blog/`, and in the sitemap, each with a future date.
- **What changed since 10-26:**
  - The publish pipeline has scheduling metadata (`status: scheduled` + `publish_date`), but nothing reads it at build time.
  - The 11-19 post is `parked` in distribution and still ships to the site.
  - The latest date moved from 11-17 to 12-03.
  - On 11-02 the feed's `<updated>` and the top card on `/blog/` read "December 3, 2026".
- **Why it matters:**
  - Google ignores future `lastmod` and `datePublished`, and lowers its trust in the site's date signals as a whole.
  - Readers and AI answer engines see dates that read as fabricated.
  - Scheduled social posts will point at pages that were indexed weeks earlier.
- **Fix (unchanged from 10-26, still the highest-leverage change on the site):**
  1. In `eleventy.config.js`: `eleventyConfig.addPreprocessor("scheduled", "md", (data) => { if (data.page.inputPath.includes("/blog/") && data.page.date > new Date()) return false; });`. An alternative is `blog/blog.11tydata.js` with `eleventyComputed` setting `permalink: false` and `eleventyExcludeFromCollections: true` when `page.date > now`.
  2. Add `schedule: - cron: "15 6 * * *"` to `deploy-docs.yml`, so a post goes live on its date.
  3. Add a future-date check to `plugins/soleur/skills/seo-aeo/scripts/validate-seo.sh` that fails if any `<lastmod>` or `article:published_time` is later than the build date.
  4. Decide what to do with the parked 11-19 post: either hold it with `permalink: false` in frontmatter, or re-date it and schedule it.
