# Technical SEO Audit — soleur.ai — 2026-10-05

**Site:** Soleur | **URL:** <https://soleur.ai> (canonical apex; `www` 301s to apex per `validate-seo.sh` canonical-host gate) | **Pages:** ~62 sitemap-eligible (index + 21 `pages/*` + 9 legal + 31 blog posts; was 49 on 2026-05-25)

**Prior audit:** [2026-05-25-seo-audit.md](./2026-05-25-seo-audit.md) — 95/100 (A) reported, 98/100 computed.

## Executive Summary

**Overall SEO score: 83/100 (B+)**. That is **-12 against the reported 95**, or -15 against the 98 the May rubric actually computed.

No P0 issues. Nothing blocks indexing or AI crawling. Robots.txt is permissive. All JSON-LD is rendered server-side in the Nunjucks templates, so none of it depends on JS. Canonicals match the sitemap host. Every template emits exactly one `<h1>`.

The score drop is **not** a production regression. It comes from three things:

1. **Two carry-over warnings were never fixed.** The homepage `og:title` is still the bare brand name, and 30 of 31 posts still use the generic `og:image:alt`. Over four months the second one has grown from a few posts to nearly the whole blog.
2. **This audit read every template.** The May audit sampled only 5 URLs. Reading all templates found issues that sample missed. The most important is a conflicting `sameAs` on the founder's `Person` entity (P1).
3. **Freshness signals have gone stale or are wrong.** Sitemap `lastmod` ignores `updated:` / `last_updated:`. 22 pages have no `date:`, so they fall back to the build-time date. The visible "Last updated" stamps on the homepage and core pages still say June 1, 2026.

**Method caveat.** This run was **source-only**. The cron containment hook blocked WebFetch, `curl`, and `npx eleventy`, so no live HTML, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, or llms.txt was fetched, and no `_site/` build was available. All findings come from `eleventy.config.js`, `_data/`, `_includes/`, and the page and post templates. The May audit was the opposite: live-only. So part of the delta is a change in method.

## Critical Issues

None at P0. The single P1 is listed first.

### P1 — `person-sameas-conflict`: Founder entity has two contradictory identity graphs

- **Where:** `plugins/soleur/docs/pages/about.njk` (ProfilePage JSON-LD, `mainEntity.sameAs`) vs `plugins/soleur/docs/_includes/blog-post.njk` (`BlogPosting.author.sameAs`).
- **What:** Both nodes share `@id: https://soleur.ai/about/#jean-deruelle`, but they point `sameAs` at different identities:
  - The About page `Person.sameAs` lists **company** profiles: `site.x` (`x.com/soleur_ai`), `site.linkedin` (`linkedin.com/company/soleur`), `site.github` (the `jikig-ai/soleur` repo), and `site.bluesky` (`soleur.bsky.social`).
  - Every BlogPosting author lists the **personal** profiles in `site.author.sameAs`: `github.com/deruelle`, `x.com/deruelle`, `linkedin.com/in/jeanderuelle`.
- **Impact:** Google and AI engines merge nodes by `@id`. The merged Person then claims to be the same entity as the Soleur organization's X, LinkedIn, and GitHub accounts. That blurs the Person and Organization entities, which is exactly what E-E-A-T author attribution needs to keep separate.
- **Also on the page:** The visible "Jean Deruelle" section on `/about/` links to "@soleur_ai on X / Twitter" plus the company LinkedIn and the repo, not to the founder's own profiles. The ProfilePage `Person` also has no `image`, although BlogPosting authors do.
- **Fix:** In `about.njk`, set `mainEntity.sameAs` to `{{ site.author.sameAs | jsonLdSafe | safe }}` and add `"image": site.url + site.author.image`. Point the visible link list at `site.author.sameAs`. The company profiles already sit correctly on the homepage `Organization.sameAs`.

## Warnings

### P2 — `sitemap-lastmod-ignores-updated`: lastmod and WebPage.dateModified ignore update fields

- **Where:** `plugins/soleur/docs/sitemap.njk` (`entry.date | dateToShort`) and `plugins/soleur/docs/_includes/base.njk` (`WebPage.dateModified` uses `page.date`).
- **What:** Blog posts carry `updated:`, which `blog-post.njk` correctly uses for `BlogPosting.dateModified` and `article:modified_time`. The sitemap and the base-layout `WebPage` node both ignore it. Affected posts:
  - `soleur-vs-polsia` and `soleur-vs-paperclip`: updated 2026-07-20, but the sitemap reports March.
  - `soleur-vs-anthropic-cowork`: updated 2026-05-21.
  - `why-most-agentic-tools-plateau`: updated 2026-04-01.
- **Same pattern on `getting-started.njk`:** it has `date: 2026-06-01` and `last_updated: 2026-09-11`. The visible stamp says Sep 11, but the sitemap and JSON-LD say Jun 1. This breaks the alignment contract documented in `_includes/page-freshness.njk`.
- **Impact:** One page emits two different `dateModified` values. Refreshed comparison posts never tell crawlers they changed, so recrawl is slower on the pages that most need it.
- **Fix:** Use `entry.data.updated or entry.data.last_updated or entry.date` in `sitemap.njk`, and `(updated or last_updated or page.date)` in the `base.njk` WebPage node. Or set `date:` equal to `last_updated:` as the partial instructs.

### P2 — `undated-pages-build-time-lastmod`: 22 pages fall back to file-created dates

- **Where:** These pages have no `date:` front matter:
  - `pages/company-as-a-service.njk`, `agentic-engineering.njk`, `ai-cmo.njk`, `ai-cto.njk`, `ai-agents-for-solo-founders.njk`, `claude-code-plugins.njk`, `solo-founder-ai-stack.njk`
  - `glossary.njk`, `goal-primitive.md`, `changelog.njk`, `community.njk`, `blog.njk`, `legal.njk`
  - all 9 `pages/legal/*.md`
- **What:** Eleventy 3 (`^3.1.5`) defaults `page.date` to the file-creation timestamp. On a fresh CI checkout that is the checkout time. So `lastmod` and `WebPage.dateModified` for these 22 URLs reset to the deploy date on every build, whether or not the content changed.
- **Impact:** Google discounts `lastmod` site-wide once it sees it is unreliable. That also weakens the accurate dates on the blog. The 7 category pillar pages also show **no visible date or byline freshness block**: they do not include `page-freshness.njk`, and only `company-as-a-service` has an inline byline.
- **Legal pages:** These show a hand-written visible date that will drift from the build-time `lastmod`. For example, `privacy-policy.md` shows "Last Updated September 28, 2026".
- **Fix:** Add `date:` and `last_updated:` to each page. On the pillar pages, also include `page-freshness.njk`. Optionally, switch to `date: git Last Modified` in a directory data file under `pages/`.

### P2 — `homepage-og-title-bare-brand`: Homepage social title is "Soleur" (carry-over from May)

- **Where:** `base.njk` builds `og:title` and `twitter:title` from `title`, which is `Soleur` in `index.njk`, never from `seoTitle`. The homepage `WebPage.name` JSON-LD is also just "Soleur".
- **Impact:** Shared homepage links and AI summarizers that read OG data get only the brand name. They miss the value proposition that the `<title>` carries ("AI Agents for Solo Founders | Every Department, One Platform").
- **Fix:** Add an `ogTitle` front-matter override, or fall back to `seoTitle` for `og:title` and `twitter:title` when `title == site.name`.
- **Status vs May:** Flagged as P1 in May. Unfixed.

### P2 — `og-image-alt-generic`: 30 of 31 blog posts use the site-wide image alt (carry-over from May)

- **Where:** The `og:image:alt` and `twitter:image:alt` default to `"Soleur - Stop Hiring. Start Delegating."` unless `ogImageAlt` is set. Only `2026-09-25-parked-vs-forgotten-ideas.md`, `pricing.njk`, and the two `compare-*` pages set it. Every other post has a post-specific `ogImage` paired with the generic alt.
- **Fix:** Add `ogImageAlt:` to each post's front matter. To keep new posts from regressing, add a `validate-seo.sh` check: fail when `ogImage` is set but `ogImageAlt` is not.
- **Status vs May:** Flagged as P1 in May. The pattern was fixed in exactly one new post.

### P2 — `llms-txt-coverage-gap`: llms.txt omits the AEO pillar and comparison pages

- **Where:** `plugins/soleur/docs/llms.txt.njk`.
- **What:** The spec structure is correct (H1, blockquote, `## Docs`, `## Optional`). But it lists only the 7 core pages plus changelog and community. It leaves out every page built specifically for answer engines:
  - `/company-as-a-service/`, `/agentic-engineering/`, `/ai-agents-for-solo-founders/`, `/ai-cmo/`, `/ai-cto/`, `/claude-code-plugins/`, `/solo-founder-ai-stack/`
  - `/glossary/`
  - the two `/compare/*` pages
  - any blog posts
- **Also:** The text hardcodes "the marketing domain's 11 specialist agents", while other counts come from `stats.*`.
- **Fix:** Add a `## Guides` section for the pillar, glossary, and comparison pages. Add a `## Blog` section looped over `collections.blog` (latest 10-20, title plus description). Derive the marketing count from data.

## Lower-Priority Findings (P3)

- **`stale-freshness-stamps`:** The homepage, `/about/`, `/agents/`, `/vision/`, `/pricing/`, `/compare/*`, and `/skills/` all show "Last updated June 1 (or June 15), 2026", which is about four months old as of today. A visible stale stamp is a negative freshness signal. Refresh `last_updated` and `date` when the copy or stats are re-verified. `site.statsLastVerified` is also `2026-04-22`.
- **`reused-og-images`:** Two posts reuse another post's OG image:
  - `2026-06-01-claude-code-plugin-vs-skill-vs-mcp.md` uses `og-best-claude-code-plugins-2026.png`.
  - `2026-06-12-loop-engineering-for-your-whole-company.md` uses `og-run-every-department.png`.

  `ai-cmo.njk` and `ai-cto.njk` explicitly set the generic `og-image.png`. Give each a distinct image.
- **`about-og-type-profile`:** `/about/` still emits `og:type=website`. The JSON-LD is already `ProfilePage`, so set `ogType: profile` to match. (Carry-over from May.)
- **`linkedin-url-inconsistency`:** `site.linkedin` (`linkedin.com/company/soleur`) and `site.linkedinCompany` (`linkedin.com/company/jikigai`) are two different company URLs. The Organization schema and footer use `jikigai`; the About page uses `soleur`. Consolidate them. This also feeds the P1 fix.
- **`twitter-creator-brand-on-posts`:** `twitter:creator` is hardcoded `@soleur_ai` site-wide. On author-bylined posts, `@deruelle` would match the BlogPosting author.
- **`feed-author-org`:** The Atom feed author in `eleventy.config.js` is `{ name: "Soleur" }`, while the posts are attributed to Jean Deruelle. Low impact, but inconsistent for feed-reading AI aggregators.
- **`faq-schema-missing-post`:** `2026-04-23-agents-that-use-apis-not-browsers.md` has no FAQ block or FAQPage schema; the other 30 posts do. `2026-05-15-skill-libraries-vs-workflow-plugins.md` mentions an FAQ only once. Check that its schema renders.
- **`cwv-unmeasured`:** There is still no Lighthouse, PSI, or CrUX data (carry-over). Fonts are not preloaded. The `font-display: swap` plus inlined critical CSS keep this low-risk, but measuring it is the only path to a 5.

## Passed Checks

- **Static structured data:** All JSON-LD lives in Nunjucks templates and renders at build time. None is injected by JS. Every interpolation goes through the `jsonLdSafe` filter, which escapes `</` and U+2028/2029.
- **JSON-LD coverage:**
  - Every page: `WebSite` + `WebPage` (`base.njk`).
  - Homepage: adds `Organization` (logo `ImageObject`, `founder` `@id`, `sameAs`, `subjectOf` NewsArticle) and `SoftwareApplication` with two `Offer`s.
  - Blog posts: `BlogPosting` with full `Person` author and publisher logo.
  - FAQPage on 30/31 posts, the homepage, About, and the pillar pages.
  - `ProfilePage` on About. The `/about/#jean-deruelle` `@id` now resolves, which closes the May P3.
- **Meta tags:** Every page has a canonical `{{ site.url }}{{ page.url }}`, a per-page `description` (all 62 sitemap templates define one), `og:locale=en_US`, `og:image` with width and height, `twitter:card=summary_large_image`, and `twitter:site`. Every referenced `ogImage` file exists under `images/`.
- **Canonical host:** One host, `https://soleur.ai`, used by `site.json`, `CNAME`, robots.txt `Sitemap:`, and the feed `base`. `validate-seo.sh` enforces the sitemap and canonical host gates.
- **robots.txt:** `User-agent: *` / `Allow: /` / `Sitemap:` with no `Disallow`. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and OAI-SearchBot are all allowed by inheritance.
- **Sitemap structure:** Built from `collections.all`, filtered to trailing-slash canonical URLs only, so there are no `.html` redirect stubs. `404`, `sitemap`, and `llms.txt` are excluded with `eleventyExcludeFromCollections`. Every entry has `lastmod` (accuracy issues above).
- **E-E-A-T:**
  - Blog posts: visible byline with `rel="author"`, `<time datetime>`, an "Updated" stamp when `updated:` is set, and an author card with photo (explicit 96x96, lazy-loaded), bio, and credentials.
  - Core pages: `page-freshness.njk` adds a summary, a "Last updated" line, and a byline.
  - About page, legal hub, privacy and cookie policies, and a status page link are in the footer.
- **Content quality:** Exactly one `<h1>` per template (33 templates). Markdown bodies start at `##`, and there are no `####` headings anywhere. No "click here" or "read more" link text. The one `<img>` (author card) has descriptive alt text. The "Learn More" on `getting-started.njk` is an `<h3>`, not link text.
- **Core Web Vitals (source heuristics):**
  - Critical CSS is inlined, guarded by the `cq-eleventy-critical-css-screenshot-gate`.
  - The full stylesheet loads async through a CSP-hashed preload swap with a `<noscript>` fallback.
  - Both webfonts use `font-display: swap`. Plausible loads `async`. There is no large JS bundle.
  - The LCP element is text.
- **Technical SEO:** `<html lang="en-US">`, UTF-8 charset, viewport, strict CSP with `upgrade-insecure-requests`, an Atom feed `<link rel="alternate">`, and a favicon.
