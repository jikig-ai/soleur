## Method and limitations

- **No live fetch was possible.** This cron environment blocks both WebFetch and curl (`cron-containment: tool class not permitted`). The audit reads the Eleventy source that deploys to soleur.ai, in `plugins/soleur/docs/`. This is the same content that renders on the live site. However, (a) templated values such as `{{ stats.agents }}` were not resolved to their rendered numbers, and (b) I could not confirm that the deployed site matches `main`. The next audit should run somewhere WebFetch is allowed, so these two gaps can be closed.
- **Presence is scored from repo evidence only.** That means the citation-monitoring tracker and the marketing knowledge base. I did not run live checks of third-party mentions or AI-engine answers.
- **Same rubric as 2026-05-25.** Weights, row labels, and the grading scale are unchanged, so the scores can be compared directly.
- **Series gap (added by the orchestrating run).** Issue #8460 (2026-09-21) reported SAP 78 (B+) and 8-component 84, flat against 2026-09-14. The 09-14 and 09-21 report files never reached the repository, so this audit could only compare against 2026-05-25. Do not read 78 → 71 as a one-week drop. Treat 71 as the new baseline.
- **Out of scope.** JSON-LD validity, meta tags, sitemaps, and llms.txt belong to the seo-aeo-analyst. Where a finding touches FAQ markup, it covers only whether the Q&A is visible on the page, not whether the schema is valid.

## Executive summary

Two things improved since May. `/getting-started/` now opens with a quotable definition of Soleur and cites three external sources: the BSL 1.1 license, Claude Code docs, and the MCP spec. That closes the top recommendation from May. A new `/glossary/` page gives 10 definitions that each open with a quotable sentence and cite an outside source. These are the best AI-extractable content on the site. `/pricing/` backs its $95K/mo comparison with Robert Half, Payscale, and Levels.fyi. The loop-engineering post (2026-06-12) is a model of named-expert attribution.

Those gains are cancelled out by three problems.

1. **Conflicting facts.** The site states Cursor's revenue two ways. Four pages give roughly $1 billion ARR, three of them as flat fact ("reached $1 billion in annual recurring revenue"). The Soleur vs Cursor post's FAQ says Cursor has "$2B+ ARR" and presents it as fact. Answer engines lose trust in a source when its pages contradict each other. The brand guide has banned stating competitor ARR as fact since 2026-07-20 (#6768).
2. **Carryover fixes not applied.** The May fixes for `/company-as-a-service/` were never made. The page still says "dramatically more productive" and still has a "What is the leading CaaS platform?" FAQ.
3. **Presence was re-scored and fell.** The homepage "As seen in: Inc.com" strip links to an article about Dario Amodei's prediction, not coverage of Soleur, so it should not have earned Presence credit in May. The citation-monitoring tracker set up on 2026-06-15 has never been run: all 32 cells still say `TBD`.

Overall: Structure went up, Authority dipped slightly, and Presence dropped. The net change is −2.
