## Critical Issues

### P0 — SEO-01: no publish gate (#11132, carried; 1 future + 1 parked post)

- **Where:**
  - `eleventy.config.js` and `plugins/soleur/docs/blog/blog.json`;
  - `.github/workflows/deploy-docs.yml`;
  - the posts `/blog/alarm-passed-every-rehearsal-never-rings/` (12-03) and `/blog/first-we-made-green-mean-something/` (11-19, parked).
- **Evidence:** Eleventy builds every template whatever its date or status. The 12-03 post is in `collections.blog`, the Atom feed, `/blog/` and the sitemap, with a future `lastmod`, a future `datePublished` and a future `article:published_time`.
- **Why the score holds even though the count fell to 1:**
  - The fall comes from the calendar again.
  - The parked 11-19 post has gone from "early" to "published without distribution", which is now a content-ops decision as well as an SEO one.
  - The next scheduled post brings the count straight back up.
- **Fix:** see the SEO-01 row. It is config-only and takes under an hour.

### P1 — SEO-02: FAQPage without visible FAQ (#9101, 5 posts)

Google's guidelines require FAQ content to be visible on the page. The 11-04 post's empty heading sits directly above the schema. The `what-your-mvp` post shows the correct `<details>` pattern.

### P1 — SEO-03: Person `sameAs` conflict (#9102)

The `/about/` ProfilePage and every `BlogPosting.author` share `@id` `https://soleur.ai/about/#jean-deruelle`, but they declare disjoint `sameAs` sets. That weakens author entity resolution, which is the core E-E-A-T signal.

## Passed Checks

- **Static structured data:** all JSON-LD renders at build time from Nunjucks, with nothing JS-injected. Every interpolation uses `jsonLdSafe`, which escapes `</` and U+2028/2029.
- **JSON-LD coverage:**
  - `WebSite` + `WebPage` on every page.
  - Homepage `Organization` (logo, `founder` `@id`, `sameAs`) + `SoftwareApplication`.
  - `BlogPosting` on all 45 posts, with a `Person` author, an `Organization` publisher and `dateModified` that honours `updated`.
  - FAQPage with a visible FAQ on 38 of 43 posts and on the core pages.
- **Meta tags:** every page has:
  - a canonical `{{ site.url }}{{ page.url }}`;
  - a page-specific description;
  - `og:locale=en_US`;
  - `og:image` width and height;
  - `summary_large_image` and `twitter:site`.
  - Posts also set `ogType: article` and `article:published_time`.
- **Canonical host:** there is a single host, `https://soleur.ai`, across `site.json`, robots.txt and the feed.
- **robots.txt (source):** `Allow: /`, with no `Disallow` and a `Sitemap:` line. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and OAI-SearchBot are allowed by inheritance.
- **Sitemap structure:**
  - Generated from `collections.all`, emitting trailing-slash URLs only.
  - The redirect-stub guard is in `validate-seo.sh`.
  - `sitemap.njk` and `llms.txt.njk` are excluded from collections.
- **llms.txt shape:** H1, blockquote, prose, `## Docs`, `## Optional`. Counts come from `stats.*`, except the hardcoded "11".
- **E-E-A-T, where correct:**
  - Posts have a byline with `rel="author"` and `<time datetime>`, and an author card with credentials and `rel="me"`.
  - Core and compare pages carry 2026-10-22 freshness stamps.
  - The footer links the legal pages, privacy policy and status page.
- **Content quality:** one `<h1>` per template, with descriptive link text.
- **Core Web Vitals (source heuristics):**
  - Critical CSS is inlined, and the stylesheet loads async through a preload swap.
  - Fonts use `font-display:swap`.
  - Plausible loads `async`.
  - There is no JS bundle, and the LCP element is text.
- **Technical SEO:**
  - `lang="en-US"`, UTF-8 and viewport are set.
  - The CSP is strict, with `upgrade-insecure-requests`.
  - The Atom feed has an alternate link.
  - There is a 404 page.
  - `deploy-docs.yml` runs `validate-seo.sh` along with the critical-CSS and screenshot gates.
- **Distribution alignment:** the 11-17 post went live and was distributed on the same day.
