# AEO Audit: soleur.ai — 2026-10-26

## Executive Summary

**Overall AEO score: 76/100 (SAP headline) -- Grade B+.** Prior (2026-10-19): 76 B+. Delta: **0**.
**8-component diagnostic: 82/100** (prior 81; delta **+1**).

Letter-grade note: the house scale is `A >= 90, B 80-89, B+ 75-79, C 60-74, D < 60`. On a plain A/B/C/D/F scale, 76 is a **C+/B- (reported as B+ on the house scale)**. The house scale is used for trend comparison.

What moved this week:

- **The model/harness story is now one sentence, used everywhere (prior N4, mostly fixed).** `/`, `/pricing/`, `/getting-started/`, `/vision/` FAQ, `/compare/soleur-vs-cursor/`, `/compare/soleur-vs-devin/` and `/community/` all say "Claude Code, Grok Build, Codex, Devin CLI, or OpenCode". The "Claude provides the intelligence" line on `/pricing/` is gone. This is the biggest entity-clarity gain in three cycles.
- **Publishing resumed, with original data (prior N2 fixed, F12 partial).** `blog/2026-10-23-agentic-verification-checklist-shipped.md` is a hub post with 8 spoke posts. `2026-10-27-audited-102-guardrails-four-portable.md` publishes a real first-party dataset: 102 checks, split 56/46, graded in a table (0/10/46 and 9/8/29). This is the first original-data content the site has shipped.
- **New risk: 8 posts are dated in the future and are almost certainly live now.** They are dated 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-17. `eleventy.config.js` has no future-date filter, `blog.json` publishes every file, and `deploy-docs.yml` builds on every push to `main`. The hub post links to all of them as "companion" posts, so they must be in the build. Readers and crawlers see publication dates up to 22 days ahead of today.
- **The new posts repeat the citation gap (F7).** 8 of 9 new posts link to no outside source. Only the hub cites Addy Osmani. The spokes link only to Soleur's own GitHub files and the waitlist.
- **`/vision/` was re-dated again (2026-10-22) and its false present-tense cards are still there (F3/N1, second time).** The page now contradicts itself. Its FAQ calls per-task model routing "the vision", while the card above says "Soleur selects the best model for each task. Claude for coding. GPT-4o for strategy."
- **Presence is flat for a fifth cycle (14/25).** The citation tracker still has 32 of 32 cells set to `TBD`. Today is the 19th Monday missed since the 2026-06-15 baseline.

Net: the entity and original-data gains are offset by the citation-free new posts, the future-dated posts, and the second unreviewed `/vision/` re-date. The SAP total is unchanged.

**Method note:** The `cron-containment` hook blocked WebFetch again (`tool class not permitted: WebFetch`), and `git log` was blocked too (`not allowlisted`). The audit ran on the Eleventy templates, markdown, and `_data/` files that generate soleur.ai, not on the rendered live site. FIXED/OPEN status was checked against current source text, not commit history. Template variables (`stats.*`) were not resolved. Off-site Presence (third-party mentions, live AI-engine answers) could not be checked. The future-dated-post finding (N5) is inferred from the build configuration. It could not be confirmed against the live `/blog/` page.
