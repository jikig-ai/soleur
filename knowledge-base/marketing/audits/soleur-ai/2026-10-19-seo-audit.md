## Critical Issues

No P0. Two P1s, both carried.

### P1 — `person-sameas-conflict` (#9102, carried)

- **Where:** `plugins/soleur/docs/pages/about.njk` (ProfilePage `mainEntity.sameAs`, and the visible `<ul>` under `#jean-deruelle`) vs `plugins/soleur/docs/_includes/blog-post.njk` (`author.sameAs`).
- **What:** Both nodes share `@id: https://soleur.ai/about/#jean-deruelle`. The About page says the founder is the same entity as the company's X, LinkedIn, GitHub repo and Bluesky. Every post says he is `github.com/deruelle`, `x.com/deruelle` and `linkedin.com/in/jeanderuelle`. When search engines merge nodes by `@id`, the person and company identities blur together.
- **Fix:** In `about.njk`, replace the four-item `sameAs` array with `{{ site.author.sameAs | jsonLdSafe | safe }}`. Add `"image": {{ (site.url + site.author.image) | jsonLdSafe | safe }}`. Render the visible link list from `site.author.sameAs`. The company profiles already sit correctly on the homepage `Organization.sameAs` in `base.njk`.

### P1 — `faqpage-without-visible-faq` (#9101, carried)

- **Where:** the 4 posts listed in the delta table.
- **What:** Each post ends with a FAQPage `<script type="application/ld+json">` whose questions never appear on the page. Google's structured-data policy requires marked-up Q&A to be visible. Without it the markup is ineligible for rich results, and at worst it can trigger a spammy-markup manual action. AI engines that cross-check schema against page text get conflicting signals.
- **Fix:** Add a visible `## Frequently Asked Questions` section with the same questions and answers to each post, or delete the schema. Also extend the FAQ gate in `plugins/soleur/skills/seo-aeo/scripts/validate-seo.sh` (the `#2948` block). Today it only checks one direction (`faq-` class present, so FAQPage required). Add the reverse check: FAQPage present, so a visible FAQ heading or `faq-` markup is required.

## Warnings

### P2 — `sitemap-lastmod-ignores-updated` (#9106, carried, partially fixed)

- **Where:** `plugins/soleur/docs/sitemap.njk` (`<lastmod>{{ entry.date | dateToShort }}</lastmod>`) and `plugins/soleur/docs/_includes/base.njk` (`WebPage.dateModified` from `page.date`).
- **Status:** `getting-started` is now aligned because its `date` equals its `last_updated`. The 4 posts with `updated:` still report their March/April publish dates in the sitemap and in `WebPage.dateModified`, while `BlogPosting.dateModified` and `article:modified_time` correctly say the later date. So one page still emits two different `dateModified` values.
- **Fix:** `sitemap.njk`: `{{ (entry.data.updated or entry.data.last_updated or entry.date) | dateToShort }}`. `base.njk`: `(updated or last_updated or page.date) | dateToRfc3339`.

### P2 — `undated-pages-build-time-lastmod` (#7180, #8468, carried, partially fixed)

- **Where:** the 21 undated templates listed in the delta table.
- **What:** With no `date:`, Eleventy 3 uses the file's creation time. On a fresh CI checkout that is the deploy time, so these URLs report a new `lastmod` on every deploy. Once Google sees `lastmod` is unreliable it discounts it site-wide, including the accurate blog dates. The 6 undated pillar pages also show no visible date. `/company-as-a-service/` now has a `date:` but still no visible "Last updated" line, because it doesn't include `page-freshness.njk` and has no `last_updated:`.
- **Fix:** Add `date:` and `last_updated:` (equal values) to each page. Include `_includes/page-freshness.njk` on the pillar pages, or add a `<time>` element to their existing `blog-post-meta` byline. Re-verify facts on `agents.njk`, `skills.njk` and the two compare pages before bumping their June stamps. Alternatively, add a `pages/pages.11tydata.json` with `"date": "git Last Modified"`.

### P2 — `homepage-og-title-bare-brand` (#2674, carried since May)

- **Where:** `plugins/soleur/docs/_includes/base.njk`, `og:title` and `twitter:title` lines; `plugins/soleur/docs/index.njk` `title: Soleur`.
- **Fix:** Use `{{ ogTitle or seoTitle or title }}` (keeping the existing suffix logic) for `og:title`, `twitter:title` and `WebPage.name`. The homepage already has a good `seoTitle`. Check the other pages that set `seoTitle` after the change: most already contain "Soleur", so they will not get a doubled suffix.

### P2 — `og-image-alt-generic` (#2557, carried since May)

- **Where:** `base.njk` falls back to `site.name + ' - ' + site.tagline`. 30 of 31 posts and all 7 pillar pages use that fallback.
- **Fix:** Add `ogImageAlt:` to each post and pillar page. Add a `validate-seo.sh` check that fails when a page's `og:image` is not `og-image.png` but its `og:image:alt` equals the site default. This is the third audit in a row with this finding; without a gate, new posts will keep repeating it.

### P2 — `llms-txt-coverage-gap` (#7181, carried)

- **Where:** `plugins/soleur/docs/llms.txt.njk`.
- **Fix:** Add a `## Guides` section for `/company-as-a-service/`, `/agentic-engineering/`, `/ai-agents-for-solo-founders/`, `/ai-cmo/`, `/ai-cto/`, `/claude-code-plugins/`, `/solo-founder-ai-stack/`, `/glossary/`, `/compare/soleur-vs-cursor/`, `/compare/soleur-vs-devin/`. Add a `## Blog` loop over `collections.blog` (newest 15-20, title plus description). Put `/goal-primitive/` and `/known-external-tools/` under `## Optional`. Derive the marketing-agent count from `agents.js` data instead of the literal "11".
