**Site:** Soleur | **URL:** <https://soleur.ai> | **Pages:** 72 sitemap-eligible (index + 22 `pages/*` + 9 legal + 40 blog posts; was 63 on 2026-10-19, +9 posts)

**Prior audit:** [2026-10-19-seo-audit.md](./2026-10-19-seo-audit.md): 83/100 (B+). This run uses the same rubric, weights and integer category scores, so the numbers compare directly.

## Executive Summary

**Overall SEO score: 78/100 (B). Delta vs 2026-10-19: -5.**

There is no P0. robots.txt is still permissive, all JSON-LD still renders server-side, every template still has exactly one `<h1>`, and nothing blocks indexing or AI crawling.

The score dropped because of one new regression. **8 of the 9 new blog posts have dates in the future** (2026-10-27 to 2026-11-17; today is 2026-10-26). Nothing in the Eleventy pipeline holds future-dated content back:

- no date filter in `eleventy.config.js`
- no `eleventyComputed` / preprocessor in `blog/blog.json`
- no scheduled rebuild in `deploy-docs.yml`

Every push to `main` therefore builds and deploys these posts **now**, each carrying a future date in:

- its visible byline
- `article:published_time`
- `BlogPosting.datePublished`
- `WebPage.dateModified`
- its sitemap `<lastmod>`
- the Atom feed

The matching distribution files in `knowledge-base/marketing/distribution-content/` are still `status: draft` with empty `publish_date`, so the posts are going out early without their planned announcements. This costs Sitemap 4 -> 3 and E-E-A-T 4 -> 3.

The new posts also add two more instances of open defect classes:

- `2026-11-04-before-your-ai-team-takes-a-recurring-job.md` has a FAQPage schema under an empty visible "Frequently asked questions" heading (#9101 class).
- The same post points `ogImage` at a file that does not exist (new defect).

**Since 10-19, none of the seven tracked issues has closed.** The main progress is freshness: home, About, Vision, Pricing and Getting Started moved to `date`/`last_updated: 2026-10-22`, and both `/compare/` pages moved from June 2026 to 2026-10-22. That closes the compare-page half of the `stale-freshness-stamps` P3.

**Method caveat.** This run was **source-only**, like the last three:

- **No live fetch.** WebFetch was refused by cron containment (`cron-containment: tool class not permitted: WebFetch`), so the live homepage, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt and blog posts were not fetched.
- **No build.** Per the task constraints, no Eleventy build was run, and there is no `_site/` in the checkout, so `validate-seo.sh` was not run.

"Deployed" claims below follow from source on `main` plus the `deploy-docs.yml` trigger (push to `main` on `plugins/soleur/docs/**`). They are not confirmed against production.

### P1: `future-dated-posts-published-early` (NEW)

**Where.** Eight posts in `plugins/soleur/docs/blog/`:

| File | `date:` |
|---|---|
| `2026-10-27-audited-102-guardrails-four-portable.md` | 2026-10-27 |
| `2026-10-29-property-test-cannot-fail-teaches-nothing.md` | 2026-10-29 |
| `2026-11-03-first-property-test-found-real-bug.md` | 2026-11-03 |
| `2026-11-04-before-your-ai-team-takes-a-recurring-job.md` | 2026-11-04 |
| `2026-11-05-map-that-cant-drift.md` | 2026-11-05 |
| `2026-11-10-test-the-migration-not-just-the-schema.md` | 2026-11-10 |
| `2026-11-12-map-that-only-records-what-the-agent-saw.md` | 2026-11-12 |
| `2026-11-17-flaky-test-passed-on-retry-quarantine.md` | 2026-11-17 |

**Evidence.**

- `eleventy.config.js` has no date filter or preprocessor. `blog/blog.json` only sets `tags`, `layout`, `permalink`, `ogType`, with no `eleventyComputed`.
- A grep for `future|eleventyComputed|publishDate` across `plugins/soleur/docs/`, `deploy-docs.yml` and `validate-seo.sh` finds no publication gate.
- Eleventy publishes every template whatever its date, so each post is in `collections.blog` and `collections.all` and gets an output file.
- `deploy-docs.yml` runs on every push to `main` touching `plugins/soleur/docs/**` and has no `schedule:`.
- `knowledge-base/marketing/distribution-content/2026-11-17-flaky-test-passed-on-retry-quarantine.md` is `status: draft` with an empty `publish_date`. These posts were meant to launch on their dates.

**What it emits today, per post.**

- `<time datetime="2026-11-17T00:00:00.000Z">November 17, 2026</time>` in the visible byline (`_includes/blog-post.njk`).
- A future `article:published_time`, `BlogPosting.datePublished` / `dateModified` and `WebPage.dateModified`.
- A future `<lastmod>` in `sitemap.xml`.
- Top position in the Atom feed (`collection: blog, limit: 20`). The feed's `<updated>` becomes 2026-11-17.
- A card dated "November 17, 2026" at the top of the "Engineering Deep Dives" section of `/blog/`.

**Why it matters.**

- **Google:** ignores future `lastmod` and future `datePublished`, and lowers its trust in the site's date signals as a whole.
- **Readers and AI answer engines:** see dates that have not happened yet, which reads as fabricated.
- **Distribution:** feed subscribers and crawlers get 8 weeks of content at once. The planned X/LinkedIn/Discord launches will then point at posts that were indexed weeks before.
- **Copy:** "the build log we published last week" in the 10-27 post is only true on its stated date.

**Fix.**

1. **Hold future posts out of the build.** In `eleventy.config.js`, add a preprocessor (Eleventy 3 API):

   ```js
   eleventyConfig.addPreprocessor("scheduled", "md", (data) => {
     if (data.page.inputPath.includes("/blog/") && data.page.date > new Date()) return false;
   });
   ```

   An equivalent alternative is a `blog/blog.11tydata.js` with `eleventyComputed` that sets `permalink: false` and `eleventyExcludeFromCollections: true` when `page.date > now`.
2. **Publish on the date.** Add `schedule: - cron: "15 6 * * *"` to `.github/workflows/deploy-docs.yml` so a post goes live on its date without a manual push.
3. **Gate it in CI.** Add a check to `plugins/soleur/skills/seo-aeo/scripts/validate-seo.sh` that fails when any sitemap `<lastmod>` or `article:published_time` is later than the build date.
4. **Alternative to steps 1-3:** if the posts were meant to be live now, re-date them to their actual merge date and adjust any relative-time wording.
