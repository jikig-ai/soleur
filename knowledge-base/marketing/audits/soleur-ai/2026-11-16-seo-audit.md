## Critical Issues

### P0 — SEO-01: `future-dated-posts-published-early` (#11132, carried; 3 posts)

- **Where:** `eleventy.config.js`, `blog/blog.json`, `deploy-docs.yml`, and the 11-17, 11-19 and 12-03 posts.
- **Evidence:** Eleventy builds every template whatever its date. Each future post is therefore in:
  - `collections.blog` and `collections.all`;
  - the Atom feed (`limit: 20`);
  - `/blog/`;
  - the sitemap, with a future `lastmod`;
  - `BlogPosting.datePublished` and `article:published_time`.
- **Why the score did not recover even though the count fell from 8 to 3:**
  - Time passing is not a fix. The 12-03 post will still be crawlable 17 days early.
  - The parked 11-19 post has no date at which it becomes correct.
  - Each new scheduled post adds to the count again.
- **Fix:** see the SEO-01 row. This is still the highest-leverage change on the site. It is config-only and takes under an hour.

### P1 — SEO-02: `faqpage-without-visible-faq` (#9101; 11-04 now live)

The 11-04 recurring-job post is past its date and serves FAQPage schema under an empty visible heading. Google's guidelines require FAQ content to be visible on the page. Mismatches like this risk losing rich results site-wide and are a manual-action pattern. The new `what-your-mvp` post shows the right pattern to copy: `<div class="faq-list">` with `<details>` items that mirror the JSON-LD.

### P1 — SEO-03: `person-sameas-conflict` (#9102, carried)

The `/about/` ProfilePage Person and every BlogPosting author share `@id` `https://soleur.ai/about/#jean-deruelle` but declare disjoint `sameAs` sets: company profiles on `/about/`, personal profiles on posts. Entity resolution for the author (E-E-A-T) gets mixed signals.

## Warnings

- **SEO-04 (P2): reused OG cards.** Three recent posts render other posts' cards on social shares.
- **SEO-05 (P2): `lastmod` ignores `updated`.** This is a one-line template fix.
- **SEO-06 (P2): 22 undated URLs report build time.** Google learns to ignore `lastmod` site-wide when most values change on every deploy.
