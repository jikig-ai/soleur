## Questions

Each quoted sentence below is word for word from Jikigai's published Privacy Policy, GDPR Policy and Data Protection Disclosure. Where a sentence appears in more than one of them, I quote it once and name the sections. For each quoted sentence, answer "yes" (it can stay as published) or "no", with the exact change you require.

### The approval queue

#### Can Jikigai keep the approval queue switched on for every production workspace, including workspaces that Jikigai does not own, while the data protection impact screening and the standalone legitimate-interest assessment named in its published policies are not completed?

_Why I am asking: I need a yes or a no on keeping the feature on as it is today, not on a future state._

>

#### If your answer to the previous question is no, what must Jikigai complete before the approval queue is on for workspaces that Jikigai does not own?

>

#### Can this status sentence stay as published?

Privacy Policy Section 4.15, GDPR Policy Section 3.17 and Data Protection Disclosure Section 2.3(ah), each at the head of the section:

"Status as of November 9, 2026: the approval queue is switched on in production for every workspace, including workspaces that Jikigai does not own."

>

#### Can this sentence on the steps not yet completed stay as published?

Privacy Policy Section 4.15:

"The GDPR Policy names a data protection impact screening and a standalone legitimate-interest assessment as steps to come first; neither has been completed."

>

### Retention of the founder decision record

The founder decision record is the record of a founder's choice (accept, decline or request changes) on a legal text, described in Privacy Policy Section 4.17, GDPR Policy Section 3.19 and Data Protection Disclosure Section 2.3(aj). Unless I say otherwise, each sentence below appears in all three.

#### Is a fixed period of 5 years from the date of the decision a lawful retention period for this record under Article 5(1)(e)?

"5 years from the date of the decision, and then we delete the record."

>

#### Can Jikigai rely on Article 17(3)(e) together with Article 6(1)(f) to keep this record after the founder deletes their account?

"The record is not deleted when you delete your account: we keep it under Article 17(3)(e) GDPR (establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims) together with Article 6(1)(f), so statements elsewhere in this document that data is deleted on account deletion do not apply to it."

>

#### Can this extension sentence stay as published?

"If a legal claim, a complaint or an official inquiry about the record is open when the 5 years end, we keep it until that matter is closed."

>

#### Can this sentence on what the period covers stay as published?

"This covers the whole record, including any note and the marker in the third table, which is kept and deleted with the decision record it names."

>

#### Is it enough to disclose that the deletion step is not yet built, in these words?

"We have not yet built the step that deletes a record at the end of this period (the application cannot delete the record today); we will build it before any record reaches that age, and until then records are kept as they are."

>

#### Can the backup sentence stay as published, with a period that is not stated?

"Copies in database backups may last for a further period we have not measured."

>

#### Can the sentence on records of code requests stay as published?

"Records of code requests are not covered by the 5 years: those older than 30 days are deleted whenever the next request is made, so one can last longer than 30 days, and they are not deleted when you delete your account."

>

#### Can the sentence on monitoring copies stay as published?

Privacy Policy Section 4.17 (the GDPR Policy and the Data Protection Disclosure say "our Privacy Policy states 90 days" in place of the section numbers):

"The copies of a decision held by our monitoring are not part of the record and are not covered by the 5 years: Sections 5.10 and 5.14 of this Privacy Policy state 90 days for the copies held by Sentry and Better Stack, and the copy in the logs on our own servers is limited by disk space, not by time, and we have not measured how long it lasts."

>

#### Can the sentence on the free-text note stay as published?

Privacy Policy Section 4.17 (GDPR Policy Section 3.19 has the same sentence with "see Retention" in place of "see Retention above"):

"Please do not write names, email addresses or private details about yourself or anyone else in the note: it is saved with your decision and kept for as long as the decision record is kept (5 years from the date of the decision, see Retention above), even if you later delete your account. It cannot be removed on its own: it is deleted together with the record."

>

#### Can the short account-deletion entry stay as published?

Data Protection Disclosure Section 10.3(l):

"Not deleted on account deletion; kept under Article 17(3)(e) with Article 6(1)(f) (Section 2.3(aj)), for 5 years from the date of the decision and longer only while a legal claim, a complaint or an official inquiry about it is open; the step that deletes a record at the end of that period is not yet built."

>

#### Once you have answered the questions above, is the retention wording final, so that I can remove the draft markers these sections carry?

>
