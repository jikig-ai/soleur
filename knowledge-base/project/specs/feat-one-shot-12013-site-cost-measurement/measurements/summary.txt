METHOD
Fresh clone per reading, detached at the tree, with the uncommitted stamp hook (rows-range-site-cost-hook.patch) applied inside the clone only. The three arms ran concurrently and standalone (nice 10, env -u CI, own TMPDIR each). A stamp is written at the end of argument parsing (0), at the gate of site k (k), and at the entry of the end-of-file gate (EOF). raw_cost of site k = the owner arm's interval k to k+1 minus the median of the two other arms' intervals over the same site (unclamped); reps are aggregated by median (with two reps the median of two is their mean); unconditional_s of an arm = its wall minus its owned raw_cost. Raw files: raw/. Table: site_costs.tsv (by-rep values: site_costs.by_rep.tsv). Rebuild: python3 -I site_cost_table.py raw rebuilt.tsv.
noise_s in the table is the spread of the two non-owner skip intervals (milliseconds); it is not a between-rep measure. The between-rep noise is carried by the split-half and rep-disagreement tests below.
The table is a standalone-contention measurement scaled to the pool by a fitted factor; it is relative cost. No pooled gain is claimed from it.
site_cost_run.sh was hardened after readings r4 and r5 were taken (exit trap that stops the arms on INT/TERM/HUP/QUIT, in-run foreign-run sightings that make a reading DISTURBED, a fixed lock path, LC_ALL=C, an arm timeout, the commit resolved once, a sampler-liveness and tick-rate requirement: about one tick per 15 s of the arms' run, 2 ticks of slack); the readings ran the earlier version, committed in b21d8dd51. The hardened version was exercised on its refusal paths (load, lock, bad tree), on a kill test (TERM mid-reading left no arm running and the lock free) and on throwaway smoke readings (rows 1-1, on the 473-site tree: 475 stamp lines, rc 0; the in-run sampler flagged a real sibling worktree's test run, so that reading was DISTURBED as designed and its own clone was not flagged); it has not produced a full three-arm reading.
Limits of the guard, as stated in the runner: it sees only test-all.sh and replay-arm runs spelled `bash scripts/test-all.sh`, `bash */scripts/test-all.sh` or `bash plugins/soleur/test/ci-pr-affected-replay.test.sh`, for processes whose cwd it can read, sampled every 15 s (a shorter run can fall between ticks); other CPU load shows only as max_load1_during, which does not gate. A clean verdict is therefore evidence about those runs, not about the host.

REAP LATENCY (ruling F4 pricing): unpriceable on the committed rows
In scripts/test-all.sh the (NNNms) of the "[run] ... done" line is _pj_end minus _pj_start in _pj_fmt_done, and _pj_end is stamped when the parent reaps the worker (_pj_reap), so it already contains any reap delay. The worker-side row of TEST_TIMING_LOG carries the same figure: comparing the two for every suite of both archived readings gives a difference of 0.000 s (160 and 161 suites). The committed rows therefore cannot separate a suite's own end from its reap, and the eager-reap lever of #12263 is unpriceable on them. (The 0.000 s comparison was made on the raw run.log and timing.tsv of the archived readings, which are volatile under /var/tmp/m12231 and are not committed; the committed launch-order files hold the [run] stamps only.) (A first attempt measured the parent's done stamp minus its started stamp minus the printed figure, about 13 ms median: that is print timing, not reap delay, and is not used.)

VERDICT
GATE: NO-REBALANCE (table does not support a rebalance)
Why: the table failed the stability tests of the validation (see the split-half lines: four blocks of arm b and four of arm c differ between r4 and r5 by more than 20%, and arm c's rank order differs), so the table does not support a rebalance, whatever the gain figures say. The gain figures are corroboration from the same table: the nominal-best splits put nearly all gated work into one arm, which frees the other two pool slots early, and their minimum paired gain over the 81 perturbation combinations is negative (the two candidate splits, evaluated under both pristine models, give the minimum shown in the gate lines). No search was made for a modest but robust split, so "recovers nothing robust" is not claimed; the claim is that the nominal-best split is not robust. Phase 5 (cold readings) is skipped by the plan's own rule; zero cold readings were taken.

NOTE ON THE SPLIT-HALF BLOCKS: an arm's owned sites are bucketed i*10//n into ten blocks. An earlier layout (contiguous blocks of n//10 sites plus a merged leftover) gave a different rank result for arm a (differed, now equal); the verdict is unchanged because arms b and c fail on block instability under both layouts. The rank test is layout-sensitive and strict (nearly tied blocks can swap), so arm c's DIFFERS is read as not stable enough and nothing more.

NON-CLAIMS
No pooled gain is claimed. The table is relative cost from standalone concurrent arms. Two clean reps do not make the per-site values stable enough to move a split boundary (that is what the failed stability tests say). The 40 s reading of the previous change is not re-claimed. The start-first rows price the three long suites hoisted together (ruling F4), not approval-guard1-mutation alone, and are information only.
