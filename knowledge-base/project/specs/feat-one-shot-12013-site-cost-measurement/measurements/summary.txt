METHOD
Fresh clone per reading, detached at the tree, with the uncommitted stamp hook (rows-range-site-cost-hook.patch) applied inside the clone only. The three arms ran concurrently and standalone (nice 10, env -u CI, own TMPDIR each). A stamp is written at the end of argument parsing (0), at the gate of site k (k), and at the entry of the end-of-file gate (EOF). raw_cost of site k = the owner arm's interval k to k+1 minus the median of the two other arms' intervals over the same site (unclamped); reps are aggregated by median; unconditional_s of an arm = its wall minus its owned raw_cost. Raw files: raw/. Table: site_costs.tsv (by-rep values: site_costs.by_rep.tsv). Rebuild: python3 -I site_cost_table.py raw rebuilt.tsv.
The table is a standalone-contention measurement scaled to the pool by a fitted factor; it is relative cost. No pooled gain is claimed from it.

REAP LATENCY (ruling F4 pricing): unpriceable on the committed rows
In scripts/test-all.sh the (NNNms) of the "[run] ... done" line is _pj_end minus _pj_start in _pj_fmt_done, and _pj_end is stamped when the parent reaps the worker (_pj_reap), so it already contains any reap delay. The worker-side row of TEST_TIMING_LOG carries the same figure: comparing the two for every suite of both archived readings gives a difference of 0.000 s (160 and 161 suites). The committed rows therefore cannot separate a suite's own end from its reap, and the eager-reap lever of #12263 is unpriceable on them. (A first attempt measured the parent's done stamp minus its started stamp minus the printed figure, about 13 ms median: that is print timing, not reap delay, and is not used.)

VERDICT
GATE: NO-REBALANCE (table does not support a rebalance)
Why: the table failed the split-half stability test (the rank order of the material blocks differs between r4 and r5 for arms a and c, and four blocks each of arms b and c differ by more than 20%). The splits the model rewards put nearly all gated work into one arm, which frees the other two pool slots early, and their paired gain over the 81 perturbation combinations is negative (-357 s and -366 s): arm a becomes the long pole as soon as it is a little slower. Phase 5 (cold readings) is skipped by the plan's own rule; zero cold readings were taken.

NON-CLAIMS
No pooled gain is claimed. The table is relative cost from standalone concurrent arms. Two clean reps do not make the per-site values stable enough to move a split boundary (that is what the failed stability test says). The 40 s reading of the previous change is not re-claimed.
