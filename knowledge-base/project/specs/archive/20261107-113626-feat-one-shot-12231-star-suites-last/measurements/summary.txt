## What the readings show

- The structural property holds in the T1 log: the one selected `*` suite (`scripts/test-all-capacity-signal`, about 6 s) launched with
  no other suite live, after the last non-`*` suite ended, and the run ended on it (+2072 s, wall +2081 s). In the controls it launched when
  no other suite was live as well (the pool had drained to its longest suite), and then every later registration waited behind it: counting
  suites whose start is later than the star's start in `<reading>.suites.tsv`, 50 suites (954 slot-seconds) in the disturbed control (file not kept) and 50 (827) in
  control2, none in t1.
- Idle slot-seconds fell from 2003 (control2) to 1428 (t1), but that is a difference between two runs, not a measurement of what the park caused.
  The stranded drain behind the star (about 235 s of pool phase in control2, 269 s in control) is gone. Seconds with at most one suite live
  ROSE against the clean control, 305 s to 364 s: the long suites now form a single-suite tail (the decision-challenges check "seconds with at most
  one suite live must fall against C" is met against the disturbed control, 435 s, and not against the clean one).
- The pool end is set by `approval-guard1-mutation` alone: it is the last non-`*` suite to end in every reading (its end equals the star's
  start). Its wall is 1049 s (control), 880 s (control2) and 1080 s (t1), and the mean number of other suites live while it ran roughly doubled in
  t1 (about 0.8 to 1.7), because the park moved about 35 to 50 suites into its window. One reading per arm, so that is a hypothesis for why 575
  fewer idle slot-seconds bought only 40 s of pool phase. `argv-bearer-sweep` and `destroy-ask-mutation` end well before the pool end. The
  lever #12263 (start-first for the long suites) is the one this points at.
- AC7 (claim criterion): T1's pool phase is 40 s below the faster control, under the 100 s bar. So the PR says `Ref #12231`, not `Closes`.
  The park is kept on the structural evidence (AC6 and the Phase 1 fixtures), not on this delta. "No regression" holds for the POOL PHASE only,
  against one clean control: the critical suite's own wall was 200 s longer in t1 than in control2.
- Run-to-run spread is not resolved by three readings: control and control2 differ by 263 s wall (the first was disturbed), and the clean pair
  differs by 57 s. The readings ran in a fixed order (control, control2, t1) and cold derive fell monotonically (255, 234, 216 s) although the
  scheduler cannot affect it, so warm-up favouring later readings is a live confound; quote pool phase only, and an ABBA order would be needed
  for a claim. A 40 s pool-phase difference is inside that spread. Read the T1 result as "structural property confirmed, gain not resolved on
  this diff".
