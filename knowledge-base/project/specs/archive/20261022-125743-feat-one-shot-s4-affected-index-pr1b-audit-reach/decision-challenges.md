Technical forks were ruled by the CTO agent (spawned with "do NOT use AskUserQuestion") on 2026-10-20, in one batched round, after the planner measured that reading CTO ruling 29 of the parent series literally (scan every tracked file listed in the selector row `pats`) adds 9,248 to 100,232 gap pairs, far past the ruling's own stop line of 1,244.
