| 1. R1: which rate decides row 6 ("selects at most 25% of 200 merged PRs", "selected more often"): total after the edge, glob-only, or incremental | technical | Ruled by CTO agent, 2026-10-26: TOTAL selection rate after the edge = existing edges (declared and derived) OR the candidate glob, over the 200 units. Rationale: the stop line prefers the stricter option when the evidence is thin. |
| 3. R3: duration gaps (`plugins/soleur` has no row in `scripts/suite-durations.tsv` or `scripts/suite-durations-heavy.tsv`; `plugins/soleur/test/boundary-prefix.test.sh` is `src=floor`) | technical | Ruled by CTO agent, 2026-10-26: a `floor` value is an estimate, not a timing, and does not count. Direct timing is allowed only as a labelled supplement: median of at least 5 runs on the same warm runner class the `measured` rows used, recorded as `src=direct` with N and spread, never in the `measured` column. The verdict then needs the median at or below 80% of the threshold (48 s for the glob case, 24 s for ALWAYS_ON); closer to the line stays baselined. If the runner class cannot be matched, the pair stays baselined. `plugins/soleur` is a whole `bun test` run and is likely over the limits: time it before designing anything for it. |
| 4.
