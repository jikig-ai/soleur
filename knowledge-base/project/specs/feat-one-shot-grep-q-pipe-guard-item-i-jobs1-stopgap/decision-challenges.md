## Corrections

- 2026-10-16: the CTO brief and the rulings above quoted "81 of 192 completed infra steps". The denominator was a planner arithmetic slip. The re-run of the committed instrument (`measure/run-all.sh`, `main` branch only) reads 81 killed of 269 infra steps that could deliver a verdict (30%), plus 31 runs with no verdict (cancelled or early). The numerator and every ruling are unchanged; the ratio the rulings lean on is 30%, not 42%, and the baseline argument (a broken main goes unreported for those runs) holds at either figure. The first measurement also included four early-August dry-run dispatches from feature branches (`-P 4` rows); the instrument now lists `--branch main` only, so those rows are gone and the infra `-P` column reads `1` for all 269.
