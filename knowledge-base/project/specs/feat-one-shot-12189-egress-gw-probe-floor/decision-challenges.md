# Decision challenges (plan phase, headless)

Rulings and findings persisted during planning of the fix to the #12189 soak probe (Ref #12180). The operator's three
stated defects and the pass criterion are the default and are not re-asked. The CTO was consulted twice: once for the
design, and again after a read-only measurement (two reads of the live alert listing, 14:10Z and 14:20Z on 2026-11-06)
showed that alert `updated_at` bumps about every five minutes with no apply, which falsified the first soak-origin ruling.
