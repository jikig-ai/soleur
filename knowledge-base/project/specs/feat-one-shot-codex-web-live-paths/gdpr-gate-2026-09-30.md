Finding: **GDPR-Art-6 — missing generation-column annotation**

- Severity/article: Important, Article 6.
- Location/pattern: `apps/web-platform/supabase/migrations/144_codex_auth_mode_rebind.sql`, the `auth_mode_generation` additions to `agent_engine_runs` and `agent_engine_attempts`, with no `LAWFUL_BASIS` comment in that migration.
- Why it matters: the migration does not record why this user-linked operational metadata is processed. Migration 145's neighboring annotation does not satisfy the literal per-migration check.
- Migration 144 is already applied in the shared dev migration ledger. Preserve its
  checksum; do not amend the historical migration in place. Keep this Important
  documentation gap open for an attributable CLO-reviewed record or a forward
  correction that does not rewrite applied migration history.

Finding: **GDPR-Art-6 — member ACK basis remains provisional**

- Severity/article: Important, Article 6.
- Location/pattern: migration 149's `LAWFUL_BASIS` comment for `codex_history_transfer_acknowledgments` explicitly requires CLO validation; the legal posture's “Agent-engine migration 149 amendment” repeats that limit.
- Why it matters: recording an acknowledgment does not itself establish the lawful basis for retaining the ledger or transferring conversation history.
- What to do: obtain an attributable CLO determination on the purpose, basis and scope before qualification. Keep the ledger basis described as provisional meanwhile.

Finding: **GDPR-Chapter-V — provider and mode-specific disposition unresolved**

- Severity/article: Important, Articles 28 and 44–49.
- Location/pattern: `knowledge-base/legal/compliance-posture.md`, “Vendor DPA Status”; `knowledge-base/project/specs/feat-one-shot-codex-web-rollout/clo-decision-packet.md`, “CLO assessment — 2026-09-27.”
- Why it matters: the packet records API-key and managed ChatGPT modes independently as **PENDING — no authorization**. A workspace identity or local signed-in CLI probe does not establish an authorized Web provider relationship. The historical credential statement in that packet was not rechecked here.
- What to do: qualify each mode separately with attributable account/agreement, permitted cohort/data classes, location/transfer basis, retention/erasure, billing/administrator ownership, restrictions and review date. Have CLO record the applicable provider/vendor disposition; do not infer it from another mode or public policy.
