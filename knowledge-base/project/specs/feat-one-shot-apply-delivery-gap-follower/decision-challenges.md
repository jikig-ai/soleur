and is not re-litigated. The rows below are the implementation forks that ruling leaves open, plus one User-Challenge from
plan-review. No row is an operator question; every `technical` row was ruled by the `soleur:engineering:cto` agent in this run.

| F6 Window length | technical | Ruled by CTO agent, 2026-10-23: keep 7 days; record in the ADR addendum that an untouched gap older than 7 days ages out of the issue and the drift cron backstops it - a wider window raises the page count and re-surfaces stale noise. |
